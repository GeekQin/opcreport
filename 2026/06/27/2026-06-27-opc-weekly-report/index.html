1. Core Insights This Week

Key Finding

In 2026, the One-Person Company (OPC) has transitioned from conceptual exploration to mature practice. Data shows that as of June 2025, the number of one-person limited liability companies in China has exceeded 16 million. In the first half of 2025, new registrations reached 2.86 million, a year-on-year surge of 47%, accounting for 23.8% of all new registered enterprises. This is not just numerical growth, but a fundamental shift in the entrepreneurship paradigm.

1.1 AI Toolchains Now Fully Support OPC Operations

According to the latest industry analysis, in 2026, AI tools can fully support the entire business chain operations of a One-Person Company (OPC or Solopreneur). By comprehensively utilizing AI super-employees (AI Agents), automated toolchains, and cloud services, individual entrepreneurs can build and operate micro-enterprises with unprecedented efficiency.

Core Change: AI tools have evolved from auxiliary roles to core productivity units that are programmable, collaborative, and capable of undertaking systematic tasks.

1.2 Entrepreneurship Threshold Lowered, But Challenges Remain

Advantage Analysis:

  • Extremely Low Cost: No need to build large teams; AI tools can replace certain human tasks (copywriting, coding, data analysis, etc.)
  • Efficient Decision-Making: Founder is the decision-maker, enabling rapid response to market changes
  • Technology Empowerment: AI large models give individuals enterprise-level technical capabilities

Challenges:

  • Scattered Energy: Need to juggle multiple roles (product, operations, sales, etc.)
  • Technical Depth: Core algorithm optimization and system architecture design still require profound expertise
  • Trust Building: Traffic acquisition challenges during the cold-start phase of personal branding

2. Six Monetization Paths Deep Dive

Path 1: Custom AI Agent Development for SMEs

Monetization Logic: Many SMEs have AI implementation needs but lack technical teams. Individual developers undertake projects on a contract basis.

Service Scope: Customer service agents, sales assistants, content generation tools, data processing workflows

Pricing Model: Individual projects range from thousands to tens of thousands of RMB. After gaining experience, taking 2-3 projects per month can yield considerable income.

Path 2: Developing Universal AI Agent Products with Subscription Model

Product Direction: E-commerce intelligent customer service, content creation assistants for self-media, sales follow-up automation tools

Monetization Characteristics: High upfront investment, but once validated, can achieve passive income (users subscribe monthly)

Path 3: Content Entrepreneurship Powered by AI Agents

Efficiency Improvement: AI solves the pain point of low output efficiency in content entrepreneurship

Application Scenarios: Article generation, video scripts, social media operations

Path 4: AI Consulting and Training Services

Market Demand: Traditional enterprises urgently need AI application guidance during digital transformation

Service Forms: Corporate training, online courses, one-on-one consulting

Path 5: Building Vertical Domain AI Tools

Niche Market: Develop specialized AI tools for specific industries (legal, healthcare, education)

Competitive Advantage: Domain knowledge + AI technology creates barriers

Path 6: AI-Driven Data Services

Business Model: Provide data products such as data analysis, market research, and industry reports

Technical Support: Utilize AI for large-scale data collection, cleaning, and analysis

6. Risk Warnings and Response Strategies

⚠️ Important Reminder

Although the threshold for one-person company entrepreneurship is lowered, it is by no means zero-risk. Entrepreneurs need to pay special attention to the following points:

6.1 Technical Risks

  • Over-reliance on AI: AI-generated content requires manual review to avoid error propagation
  • Rapid Technology Iteration: Need continuous learning to maintain technical sensitivity
  • Data Security: Customer data protection cannot be ignored

6.2 Market Risks

  • Intensifying Competition: Low OPC threshold leads to homogeneous competition
  • Customer Trust: Personal branding takes time to build
  • Income Volatility: Project-based income is unstable; need to establish diversified income structure

6.3 Operational Risks

  • Energy Management: Avoid burnout, reasonably allocate work and rest
  • Legal Compliance: Contract signing, tax filing, etc. require professional guidance
  • Continuous Innovation: Maintain product/service iteration to avoid being eliminated by the market
