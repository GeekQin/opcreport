Don't Blindly Chase AI Startups: Practical Guide for OPC to Return to Business Essence in 2026

Don’t Blindly Chase AI Startups: Practical Guide for OPC to Return to Business Essence in 2026

OPC One-Person Company AI Entrepreneurship Trend Weekly Report | Week 26

June 27, 2026 | Lunar Calendar June 3rd

📊 Core Insights of the Week

AI entrepreneurship is shifting from tech frenzy back to business essence. The rise of Chinese AI models brings the golden age for one-person companies (OPC). However, the key to success is no longer chasing tech trends, but building a personal traffic foundation and anchoring real user needs. This week’s report shows: OPC model has become a mature entrepreneurship paradigm; AI Agents evolve from tools to digital employees; Vibe Coding enables non-tech personnel to develop products.

I. Trend Interpretation: Three Major Shifts in OPC Entrepreneurship Logic

1. From “Information Products” to “Cognitive Leasing”

AI can instantly generate standard answers, making generic knowledge no longer valuable. In 2026, what users are willing to pay for is your real-time judgment on specific problems, your industry insights—equivalent to “renting your second brain” to them.

💡 Practical Revelation: Don’t be an AI knowledge “relay station,” be a “cognitive intermediary.” Your value lies in: understanding user-specific scenarios + filtering suitable AI tools + providing personalized solutions.

2. From “Chasing Eyeballs” to “Being Yourself”

Users are long tired of polished performances. Real, flawed, and warm ordinary people are more likely to build trust. You don’t need perfect eloquence; you just need to sincerely share professional accumulation. This “rough awkwardness” is your best moat.

📌 Case: A small AI drama team in Shenzhen used Jimeng AI to generate storyboards and Jianying AI for editing. They produced 23 episodes of AI drama with a total cost of only 12,000 yuan. In one month of launch, playback exceeded 100 million, and platform revenue exceeded 400,000. ROI: 1:33.

3. From “Fighting Alone” to “Ecological Symbiosis”

Being alone doesn’t mean working behind closed doors. Use AI tools to handle tedious topic selection, editing, and customer service, freeing up time to focus on core decisions. You are no longer fighting alone, but “partnership” with AI in entrepreneurship.

✅ Success Formula: Hard Skills (Scalpel) + AI System (One-Person Army) + Personal IP (Trust Amplifier) = Minimum Viable Entrepreneurship Model

II. Technology Frontier: AI Agent and Vibe Coding Reshape Development Paradigm

AI Agent: No Longer a Tool, But Your Digital Employee

Key Data:

  • IBM Research (November 2025): 99% of enterprise developers are exploring or developing AI Agents
  • Gartner Prediction: By 2026, 40% of enterprise applications will have built-in task-based AI Agents (less than 5% in 2025)
  • Microsoft: 100,000 enterprises have created Agents on Copilot Studio

Core Shift: AI is transitioning from “you ask, it answers” to “you state goals, it works independently.” PwC predicts: In 2026, truly productive Agent deployments will explode because we finally understand what makes a good Agent—with business metrics, central management, and traceable value.

Vibe Coding: Building Products Without Writing Code

Origin: In February 2025, former OpenAI research scientist Andrej Karpathy coined this term, describing a programming method of “using natural language to describe requirements, AI automatically generates code.”

Current Status:

  • Y Combinator: In the Winter 2026 batch of startups, 25% of codebases are 95% AI-generated
  • Vercel and Netlify’s user base exploded due to “non-programmers using AI to write code”

⚠️ Reality Check: Upwork research shows that Agent’s success rate in completing tasks independently is still not high—but the success rate of human-AI collaboration has significantly improved. Don’t mythologize “full automation,” pursue “human-AI synergy.”

III. Chinese AI Models: The Underlying Support for OPC Golden Age

Three Major Capability Leaps

1. Qualitative Leap in Content Creation Efficiency

From article writing to visual design, from video editing to voiceover production, AI tools are compressing work that previously required multi-person collaboration to one person’s fingertips. Marketing content that took a week to complete can now be drafted in hours.

2. Exponential Improvement in Customer Service Capability

Intelligent customer service systems are no longer exclusive configurations for large enterprises. Dialogue systems based on Chinese AI models can help OPCs achieve 7x24 customer response while maintaining high-quality service experience.

3. Data Analysis and Decision Support

Chinese AI models’ natural advantages in Chinese understanding and localization scenarios enable individual entrepreneurs to obtain professional business insights. From market analysis to user feedback interpretation, AI is becoming the most reliable “strategist” for OPCs.

🚀 Entrepreneurship Configuration: One person, one computer, one set of AI tools—this is becoming the most promising entrepreneurship configuration in 2026.

IV. Practical Advice: Frank’s Two Major AI Entrepreneurship Tips

Advice 1: Build a Personal Traffic Foundation

In the AI era, traffic is not the goal but the carrier of trust. You need:

  • Short videos for exposure: Use AI to quickly generate large amounts of content and test which styles are popular
  • Long videos and podcasts for deep companionship: Establish a professional image and cultivate user stickiness
  • Final monetization through deep services and “AI Avatar Subscription”: Package your professional capabilities into AI products

Advice 2: Return to Business Essence and Anchor Real User Needs

Technology is no longer the core threshold, and super individuals are not fighting alone. You need to ask yourself:

  • What real pain points does my product solve?
  • Why are users willing to pay for my services?
  • What value can I provide that AI cannot replace?

✅ Action Checklist:

  1. Find your “scalpel” skill (hard skills that solve specific problems)
  2. Amplify this skill with AI systems (content production, product delivery, customer service)
  3. Build trust through personal IP (authentic, warm, professional)
  4. Join industry and community ecosystems (gain peer connections, industry resources, and implementation cooperation)

V. Platform Support: Mexin Technology’s SoloFounder AI Entrepreneurship Coaching System

Background Data: Carta 2025 data shows that over one-third of new companies are founded by solo founders, a 53% increase in six years. The rise of the OPC (One-Person Company) model makes “one person is an army” a reality from a slogan.

Innovative Solution: Mexin Technology pioneered the AdAgent concept, completely breaking the capability boundaries and resource barriers for solo entrepreneurship. Its SoloFounder AI entrepreneurship coaching system transforms personal skills into mature business models through a 7-step implementable process.

Core Value: Solo entrepreneurship is no longer fighting alone but having an AI “strategist” for full-process coaching.

VI. Eight Directions for Making Money with AI in 2026 (the 5th is seriously underestimated)

Direction 1: AI Video, Time-Difference Strategy

Core Logic: The dividend is not in “making videos” but in “doing it earlier than others.” When a new gameplay just emerges, platform algorithms promote such content, users find it fresh, and the supply side hasn’t rolled up yet.

Tech Stack: Jimeng generates digital humans + DeepSeek writes scripts + Jianying edits, the entire process takes ten minutes.

Money-Making Key: It’s not about doing better than others but faster than others. If you discover a new gameplay one week earlier and run through the process three days earlier, that’s your window.

Direction 2: Vertical Niche Accounts

Strategy: Generalized content tracks are already rolled to the ceiling, but vertical fields still have plenty of blanks. You don’t need a million followers; you only need 1,000 precise followers.

Example: Instead of making an “AI popularization account,” make an account like “AI teaching middle-aged and elderly people to do health exercises” or “AI helping small and micro enterprises do financial and tax reports.”

📌 Practical Case: Conducted many training sessions at the Federation of Disabled Persons, teaching the most vertical scenarios: digital humans for anti-fraud propaganda, DeepSeek for writing community notices, AI painting for holiday posters.

VII. Technology Trends: Three Major Paradigm Shifts in AI Development in 2026

1. From Next Token Prediction to Next-State Prediction

The training objective of large models is upgrading from “guessing the next word” to “predicting the next state,” marking the rise of world models. If the Transformer architecture is the cornerstone of the AI 1.0 era, then the world model is likely the only way to AGI (Artificial General Intelligence).

2. From Virtual Intelligence to Embodied Intelligence

The carrier of AI is no longer limited to screens but extends to physical entities such as robots and autonomous vehicles, beginning to interact with the real world. Embodied intelligence is breaking away from laboratory demonstrations and entering the industry screening and implementation stage. Humanoid robots will break through demos in 2026 and shift to real industrial and service scenarios.

💡 Business Opportunity Tip: The “selling shovels” business heats up first—embodied data. Training embodied models with general generalization capabilities requires high-quality training data. This is the iron law of the AI circle: the first to make money are always the “shovel sellers.”

3. From Single Intelligence to Swarm Intelligence

The capability of a single AI Agent is limited, while a multi-agent system (MAS) composed of multiple Agents will explode with unprecedented productivity through collaboration. These three trends will collectively define the new pattern of the AI 2.0 era.

VIII. Summary and Outlook: The Golden Age of OPC Has Arrived

Core Conclusions:

  • AI entrepreneurship is shifting from tech frenzy back to business essence
  • The rise of Chinese AI models brings the golden age for one-person companies
  • Key to success: Build personal traffic foundation + anchor real user needs
  • Technology trends: AI Agents become digital employees, Vibe Coding lowers development thresholds
  • Business model: From “information products” to “cognitive leasing,” from “chasing eyeballs” to “being yourself”

Next Week’s Focus:

  • Stanford HAI Annual Prediction: The era of AI evaluation has arrived, the era of evangelism is past
  • Opportunities in the upstream of the embodied intelligence industry chain (embodied data)
  • AI job market: Opportunities and traps for ordinary people under the 5 million gap
