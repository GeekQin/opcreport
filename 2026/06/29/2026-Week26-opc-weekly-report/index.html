📌 Core Insights This Week

In Week 26 of 2026, the OPC (One-Person Company) AI startup wave continues to intensify across China. Multiple cities have rolled out intensive support policies — from Shenzhen’s billion-yuan fund to Hainan Free Trade Port’s 8 million yuan park rewards, from Huaqiangbei supply chain to Qingdao’s AI industry ecosystem summit. The “Super Individual” has moved from concept to large-scale implementation. The most noteworthy signal this week: the proportion of solo founders has surged 53% in six years, and an AI Agent’s daily task capacity equals 3-5 full-time employees at less than 1/10 the cost — the business singularity of “one-person army” is accelerating.

🏛️ Policy Updates

Shenzhen Releases OPC Startup Ecosystem Action Plan (2026-2027)

Shenzhen’s “Artificial Intelligence OPC One-Person Company Startup Ecosystem Leader Action Plan” officially launched, targeting 10+ OPC communities of at least 10,000 sqm each by 2026-2027, cultivating 1,000 high-growth AI startups. Unprecedented policy support: up to 1 million yuan in startup capital, 100,000 yuan talent settlement subsidies, 10 million yuan in “Training Power Coupons” for R&D, plus low-cost workspace and affordable housing.

Hainan Free Trade Port AI OPC Special Policy Takes Effect June 20

Hainan’s four-department joint policy for AI OPC one-person companies officially went live, valid through end-2028. Key highlights: 1-3 premium OPC eco-communities selected annually with up to 8 million yuan one-time financial rewards per park; combining FTP tax, cross-border, and venue benefits with a “one core, two poles, multi-point” development layout.

Tongzhou District, Beijing Issues First OPC One-Person Company Policy — Up to 2 Million Yuan Support

Beijing’s Tongzhou District released its first OPC one-person company special policy, covering computing coupons, model coupons, and talent apartments — up to 2 million yuan in total support. This marks the Northern market’s formal entry into the OPC policy competition arena.

Shanghai Lingang Super Individual Economy Summit: 300K/500K RMB Sales Milestone Grants

The 2026 Shanghai Super Individual Economy Summit opened in Lingang, stacking with the Lingang New Area’s “Super Individual” economic measures: up to 500,000 yuan one-time grants for teams reaching 3 million or 5 million yuan in annual sales; new 10% subsidies on AI account subscriptions and model API call fees, capped at 500,000 yuan.

Guangdong Province AI OPC Innovation Action Plan (2026-2028)

Guangdong Province released its “Support for AI OPC Innovation Development Action Plan (2026-2028)”, targeting 100 AI OPC eco-communities and 1,000 benchmark enterprises by 2028. Policies cover computing coupons, public computing platforms, high-quality datasets, and cross-border scenario deployment.

🌐 Ecosystem & Trends

Solo Founder Ratio Surges 53% in 6 Years — One-Person Company Goes Mainstream

Data shows that over one-third of new companies are founded by solo founders — a 53% surge in six years. AI tools have made “one-person army” a reality: one AI Agent processes daily tasks equivalent to 3-5 full-time employees at less than 1/10 the cost. 2026 is being called “Year One of Digital Employees.”

WAIC OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge: World’s First Super Individual Top Incubator

The World Artificial Intelligence Congress (WAIC) jointly launched the OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge with OpenCSG — the world’s first top-tier incubation platform exclusively for super individuals and AI entrepreneurs. Shanghai explicitly aims to build a “rainforest-style” developer ecosystem, attracting super entrepreneurs, one-person companies, and frontier deployment engineers.

Qingdao: AI Empowerment Application & OPC Industry Ecosystem Summit, OPIE Platform Lands

Qingdao hosted the AI Empowerment Application & OPC Industry Ecosystem Summit, with the OPIE Solo AI Entrepreneur Platform officially unveiled. The platform builds four core modules: data governance, computing market, model training & inference, and AI Agent marketplace — backed by Sidus-AI Tianzhi Galaxy AI Factory. An on-site service station unveiling ceremony was held.

Suzhou: Top Projects Get 1M RMB Startup Capital, 30+ OPC Communities Planned

Suzhou offers top projects up to 1 million yuan in startup capital, planning to build 30+ OPC communities by 2028. Suzhou Wujiang has created a dedicated “Dragon Lobster (OpenClaw) Service Zone” with free OpenClaw-style agent deployment services, launching a 2 billion yuan AI direct investment fund, 10 billion yuan dedicated credit, and up to 1,500 yuan/month housing subsidies for up to 3 years.

National Super Individual Wave: 12 Million+ Chinese Entrepreneurs Enter OPC Track

From Beijing Zhongguancun to Wuhan Guanggu to Shanghai Lingang, over 12 million Chinese individual entrepreneurs are flooding into the OPC track. One person, one computer, one AI system — complete business closed loops from product R&D to content creation to marketing deployment. OPC’s core architecture: 1 core decision-maker + N AI digital employees + ∞ global collaboration network.
