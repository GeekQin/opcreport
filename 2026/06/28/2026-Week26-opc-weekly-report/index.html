Core Overview of This Week

In the 26th week of 2026, the OPC (One-Person Company) track continues to show explosive growth: local policy support has been strengthened, AI agent tools have further lowered the threshold for entrepreneurship, multiple benchmark cases of solo entrepreneurship have emerged, and mainstream monetization paths have gradually been validated. The entire track has entered a new stage from “concept verification” to “large-scale implementation”.

Policy and Technology Drive OPC into Explosive Growth Period

1. Intensive Landing of Local Policies, OPC Becomes Key Support Direction

This week, multiple local governments in China have issued special support policies for OPC:

  • Guangdong Province released the Action Plan for Supporting the Innovative Development of AI OPC (2026-2028), clearly stating that by 2028, 100 AI OPC ecological communities will be built, 1,000 benchmark OPC enterprises will be cultivated, and preferential policies such as computing power coupon subsidies and cross-border layout support will be provided.
  • At the 2026 International Innovation, Entrepreneurship and Creative Ecological Conference, Nan’an District of Chongqing announced that it will build 20 OPC offline communities with high standards, providing 3,000 exclusive free workstations to provide a low-cost and efficient development environment for OPC entrepreneurs.

2. Technology Threshold Continues to Decline, Non-Technical Personnel Can Also “Start a Business Alone”

2026 is called the “first year of digital employees” by the industry. AI agent tools represented by OpenClaw (commonly known as “lobster”) have further matured, completely breaking the technical barriers of traditional programming:

  • Technical “novices” do not need to learn programming languages. They only need to have a natural language conversation to transform vague entrepreneurial needs into operable commercial applications.
  • AI is no longer just a tool to assist in writing copy and making posters, but has evolved into a “virtual team” that works 7×24 hours without rest, covering the entire business process of product development, marketing promotion, and customer service.

Benchmark Cases of This Week: One-Person Company Can Also Get Financing and Earn High Salary

1. Technology Track: Seede AI Completes Millions of Dollars in Financing, Solving AI Design Pain Points

Chengdu AI visual design tool Seede AI, as a typical one-person company model project, disclosed this week that it has completed the first round of financing of millions of dollars. Founder Long Yi launched the project in the middle of 2024 in the one-person company model, and completed the entire process of product R&D and early operation only by himself:

  • The product relies on the exclusive rendering engine and visual description language, jumps out of the mainstream text-to-image technology route, and solves the industry pain points such as AI design text confusion, non-fine editing of finished products, and convergence of aesthetics.
  • At present, the product has more than 300,000 registered users and more than 10,000 paying users, mainly providing visual creation services such as PPT, posters, and long information pictures for e-commerce, small and medium-sized enterprises, and workplace people.

2. Service Track: Ordinary Entrepreneurs Earn More Than 10,000 Yuan a Month Relying on AI Services

Multiple cases of one-person companies of ordinary entrepreneurs have attracted attention this week:

  • Lei Zhenpeng, a 27-year-old young man from Wuhan, once set a record of 100,000 yuan in realization in a week by teaching customers to build AI digital employees (“raising lobsters”).
  • A part-time programmer takes orders for OpenClaw deployment on weekends, charging 100-200 yuan per time for remote installation, and 400-500 yuan for local door-to-door service, and the monthly income easily exceeds 10,000 yuan.
  • Zhengzhou entrepreneur Huang Tao trained the AI assistant “Tomato” in 1 hour, which helped him complete topic planning, article writing, layout design and even contract drafting. At present, he operates 7 WeChat official accounts at the same time, and the content creation cost is reduced by 90%.

The following tools are all OPC entrepreneurship adaptation tools that have been updated or have high heat this week, covering the entire chain of development, design, and content:

  • Development: Cursor (AI programming assistant, supports natural language generation code), Claude Code (AI code debugging tool), OpenClaw (AI agent building tool, no code foundation required)
  • Design: Seede AI (AI visual design tool, supports fine editing), Midjourney V6 (text-to-image tool, further improved image quality)
  • Content: ElevenLabs (AI voice generation tool, supports multiple languages and timbres), Runway ML (AI video generation tool, supports text-to-video)
  • Operation: FounderWay (AI entrepreneurship assistance tool, provides pitch deck optimization, market analysis and other functions)

Pit Avoidance Guide of This Week

Combined with the industry dynamics of this week, 3 pit avoidance suggestions for OPC entrepreneurs:

  • Avoid blindly following the popular track, give priority to the vertical field that you are familiar with and have resource accumulation, for example, if you are familiar with e-commerce, do AI tools related to e-commerce, and if you are familiar with education, do AI services in the education field.
  • Pay attention to the copyright issue of AI-generated content, and must review the copyright ownership of the content before commercial use to avoid infringement disputes.
  • Do not over-rely on AI, core strategic decisions, customer communication, and product quality control still need to be completed manually. AI is only an auxiliary tool and cannot replace your core judgment.
