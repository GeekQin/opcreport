📖 Deep Analysis: The Essence of One-Person Companies

The essence of a one-person company is the massive external extension of individual capability through technological leverage. AI tools enable one person to maintain daily updates across multiple social media accounts and content platforms, with production costs approaching zero.

The popularization of open-source models like DeepSeek further lowers barriers. Ordinary users no longer need complex prompt engineering; AI can independently understand questions and spontaneously perform reasoning, making the new “one person + AI = one team” work model a reality.

Individual workers are no longer solitary strugglers, but super individual households working closely with intelligent partners.

⚠️ Entrepreneurship Reminders

Invest carefully, don’t blindly follow trends.

  • While AI tools have lowered entrepreneurship barriers, competition is also intensifying
  • Choose a “small enough” entry point to reduce development difficulty and market validation costs
  • Release early, iterate quickly, continuously monitor user feedback
  • Customer support is critical; timely responses help gain user loyalty
