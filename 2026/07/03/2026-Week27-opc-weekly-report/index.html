⚠️ Challenges & Risks: Three Major Traps of OPC Model

1. Tech Dependency Risk

OPC highly depends on AI tools and platforms. Once the tech route changes or service interrupts, business continuity may be affected. Recommendation: Multi-cloud deployment, open-source tool backup, core capabilities self-controlled.

2. Compliance & Legal Risk

As a new organizational form, OPC has legal gray areas in intellectual property, credit management, business compliance, etc. Recommendation: Utilize entrepreneurship mentoring services provided by OPC communities, preemptive professional guidance, and bridge compliance shortcomings.

3. Market Competition Intensification

As OPC thresholds lower, market competition will intensify sharply. OPCs that simply rely on “AI tool cost reduction” will lose competitive advantage. Future OPC’s core competitiveness lies in: industry insight, productization capability, user demand understanding.
