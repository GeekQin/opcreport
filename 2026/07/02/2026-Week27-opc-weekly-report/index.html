🌟 Seven Government Departments Jointly Issue 3-Year Platform Economy Action Plan

On June 18, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, along with six other central departments, officially released the “Action Plan for Promoting Coordinated Development of Large, Medium, and Small Enterprises in the Platform Economy (2026–2028).” The policy explicitly supports cultivating AI-driven platform-type entrepreneurship entities, giving the strongest policy endorsement to OPC (One-Person Companies) and super individuals.

🏝️ Hainan Free Trade Port AI OPC Special Policy Officially Effective June 20!

Hainan Province’s four departments jointly issued an AI OPC special support policy, effective until December 31, 2028. Key highlights:

  • Up to ¥8 million one-time incentive for high-quality OPC eco-communities (1-3 selected per year)
  • Rent-free work desks for qualifying entrepreneurs
  • AI computing power consumption vouchers subsidizing token costs
  • Up to ¥10 million unsecured credit loans with 1% annual interest rate subsidy

Source: Cnblogs Corporate Blog | 2026-06-07

  • Personal entrepreneurs: Up to ¥500,000 entrepreneurship guarantee loan

⚡ Industry Trends & New Directions

🤖 AI Agents: One Person = One Army

AI tools have evolved from assistants to programmable, collaborative, system-level productivity units. One AI agent handles the daily workload equivalent to 3-5 full-time employees at less than 1/10th the cost. The Vibe Coding market has reached $4.7 billion, with 41% of all global code now AI-generated and 92% of developers using AI daily.

Using tools like Claude Code and OpenClaw, entrepreneurs are becoming “super individuals,” independently building commercial-grade applications across verticals: AI web series, smart wearables, AI+HR platforms, and enterprise smart sites.

Source: CSDN Blog / OpenCSG | 2026-06-26

🏆 WAIC × OpenCSG: World’s First OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge Launches

The World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC), in partnership with OpenCSG, launched the OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge — the world’s first top-tier incubation platform specifically designed for super individuals and AI entrepreneurs. This is not just a competition; it’s a complete ecosystem for discovering potential projects, linking capital resources, and nurturing future unicorns.

Shanghai explicitly announced plans to build a “tropical rainforest” developer ecosystem, attracting super entrepreneurs, one-person companies, and frontier engineers — positioning itself as “the city that understands developers best.”

Source: OpenCSG | 2026-06-30
