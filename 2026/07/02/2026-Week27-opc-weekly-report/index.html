🔥 This Week’s Headlines

1. OPC Registrations Surpass 16 Million, 47% YoY Growth

Industry reports show China’s one-person company registrations have reached 16 million, representing one-quarter of all registered enterprises. In Shenzhen’s Nanshan district, one office building houses 200 companies — none with a second employee. AI is driving the cost of “doing” to near zero while pushing the value of “knowing what to do” to the ceiling.

2. Anthropic CEO Predicts First $1B One-Person Company in 2026

Dario Amodei publicly predicted that 2026 will see the first one-person company valued at $1 billion. The core OPC formula — Carbon intelligence (Human) + Silicon execution (AI) = Complete company — is being validated by entrepreneurs worldwide.

3. AI Agent Explosion Year: Global Market $12.7B–$21.5B

Gartner forecasts the 2026 global AI Agent market at $12.7 billion, IDC at $16.2 billion, and McKinsey at $21.5 billion. China’s enterprise AI Agent market is projected at RMB 13.53 billion, growing 70%+ YoY. 2026 has been defined as “the year of agent deployment at scale.”

🎯 Hot Tracks for OPC Entrepreneurs

Track 1: AI Video — First-Mover Advantage

A Shenzhen team produced 23 episodes of AI anime series using Jimeng AI (storyboards) and Jianying AI (synthesis) for a total cost of only RMB 12,000. In one month, the series achieved 100 million+ views and RMB 400,000+ in platform revenue — a 1:33 ROI. The key to AI video is not quality alone but speed: discovering new formats one week before the crowd.

Track 2: Niche Micro-Accounts

General content is saturated, but vertical niches remain wide open. Accounts like “AI teaches seniors health exercises” outperform generic AI education accounts for monetization. The 2026 keyword is vertical, vertical, vertical.

Track 3: AI Agent Platforms & Development

The 2026 AI Agent landscape shows a “duopoly + niche supplement” pattern: LangChain/LangGraph dominates enterprise, OpenClaw leads personal productivity and SMB. Internet giants (Alibaba Bailian, ByteDance Coze, Tencent Yuanqi) are pivoting from consumer products to government/enterprise markets.

Track 4: Traditional Industry AI Retrofit

From solar to steel, from apparel to lighting — traditional industries are being AI-retrofitted into new money printers. A Qingdao tech company runs full AI enablement with just 4 core team members. Every traditional business owner should ask: Can my old business get an AI engine?

Track 5: Cross-Border E-Commerce & Overseas Services

AI Listing factories generate trilingual listings in 30 seconds with A9 algorithm optimization. OPC solopreneurs can now run full-stack operations on Amazon, TikTok Shop, and other global platforms.

Track 6: Prompt Engineering & AI Skill Marketplace

“Knowing how to use AI” means “knowing how to command AI.” Prompt engineers have become an emerging profession. The OPC Agent Skill Marketplace (yzt.yess.net.cn) is now live, facilitating AI skill product trading and monetization.

🔬 Deep Tech Trends

Trend 1: From Perceptual to Cognitive & Action Intelligence

Three paradigm shifts in 2026: Next Token Prediction → Next-State Prediction (World Models), Virtual → Embodied AI, Single → Swarm Intelligence (Multi-Agent Systems).

Trend 2: Multimodal AI Goes Fully Practical

Multimodal LLMs break text/image/audio boundaries. Midjourney, Sora, Runway give solopreneurs professional-grade content production. AI evolves from passive response to active decision-making.

Trend 3: Lightweight Deployment + On-Device AI

Small models run on phones and PCs without cloud connectivity, drastically reducing AI costs for OPCs.

Trend 4: Multi-Agent Collaboration Systems

From single Agent to Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), unprecedented productivity through collaboration. Octo, LangChain, OpenClaw are building the infrastructure for the “Agent Internet.”

⚠️ Risk Alert · Stay Grounded

Nearly 70% of Micro-Entrepreneurs Can’t Sustain AI Usage

Despite growing AI tool adoption, nearly 70% of micro-entrepreneurs fail to sustain use after initial trials. The cost-capability gap remains the core barrier.

The Real Moat: Professional Depth, Not Breadth

AI makes content, code, and design generation costs approach zero. Relying purely on “AI-produced content” is no longer enough. Your moat must be industry experience, network, and deep domain cognition.

Window Is Closing on Hot Tracks

AI video, prompt engineering, and other hot tracks have attracted massive entrants. Every month of delay doubles competitive pressure. Speed is everything.
