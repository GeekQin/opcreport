📌 National Standard Breaks Ground: First AI OPC Terminology Standard Officially Released

China’s first collective standard for AI OPC terminology officially released. For the first time, a clear national definition has been established:

AI OPC (Artificial Intelligence One-Person Company): A company led by 1 core natural person with generally no more than 10 employees, primarily engaged in AI technology research, development, application, or services. The core individual uses personal brand and professional capabilities as core assets, leveraging intelligent agents (AI agents) to achieve independent operations and scalable delivery.

Key evolution from the April 2026 draft: definition standard adjusted from “shareholders ≤3, employees ≤20” to the more precise “1-person leadership, ≤10 employees” — marking OPC’s formal transition from policy exploration into a regulated phase.
