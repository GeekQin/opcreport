Important Reminder: Although the OPC model is promising, it is not suitable for everyone. Common challenges include:

  • Technical Debt Risk: Over-reliance on AI-generated code may lead to maintenance difficulties

  • Compliance Risk: Copyright and privacy issues of AI-generated content remain to be clarified
  • Market Competition: Lower OPC barriers mean more intense competition
  • Income Volatility: Weak risk resistance of solo companies requires good financial planning
