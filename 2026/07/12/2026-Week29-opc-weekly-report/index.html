6. Risks & Challenges: The Reefs Beneath the Wave

⚠️ Hard realities to face:

  • “One-person company” ≠ “one person doing everything.” You still need office space, compute, finance/legal compliance, and supply-chain collaboration.
  • Only three types actually make money: technical experts playing a lower-difficulty game, vertical-niche subscription businesses, and strong personal brands. “Wild” operators routinely collapse from wrong tool choices (2× dev cycles), fuzzy branding (audience churn), or platform-policy shifts (overnight zeroing).
  • Org capability is the ceiling. One founder cut a 30-person team to 3 while keeping output — but the “super-individual” bottleneck is decision quality and energy allocation, not technology.
  • Compliance & security. Agent autonomy credibility (e.g., the decision-credibility quantification framework from SITS2026), data privacy, and the AI Law‘s on-premise deployment requirement are becoming hard gates for production-grade rollout.

  • Advice 1 — Validate a “chargeable problem” before registering a company. Right order: find a problem → sell a service → close one small loop → then standardize a product.
