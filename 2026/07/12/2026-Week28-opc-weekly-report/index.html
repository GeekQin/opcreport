Key Insight

2026 is dubbed the “Year One of One-Person Companies.” AI tools have drastically lowered the barrier to solo entrepreneurship. The proportion of new companies founded by single founders has surged from 23.7% in 2019 to 36.3% in H1 2025—a 53% increase in six years.

🏛️ Policy Updates

National-Level Support

In June 2026, seven ministries including MIIT jointly issued a document explicitly stating “accelerating the cultivation of AI OPCs (AIOPC).” Shanghai, Jiangsu, Guangdong, and other regions have incorporated OPC into their “15th Five-Year Plan.”

💡 Workflow-First Approach

For OPCs, AI selection’s main battlefield isn’t model leaderboards—it’s whether AI can be embedded into real delivery chains including design, writing, teaching, R&D, customer service, and data organization. What shortens the chain is productivity; what’s only a capability demo is mostly hype.

⚠️ Market Warning: 90% Eliminated After Boom

Despite the “Year One of OPC” narrative in 2026, the wave is receding fast:

Failure Reason Percentage
Too heavy on actions, too light on validation 35%
Lack of differentiation 25%
Weak monetization ability 22%
Tool hoarding vs. business focus 18%

Key Lesson: Registering a company ≠ building a business. Validate first, formalize later.

🎯 Top 5 AI Business Directions for 2026

  1. AI Video — Time advantage strategy: Algorithm pushes new content formats before supply floods in
  2. Vertical Micro-Accounts — “AI teaching elderly to do health exercises” or “AI helping SMBs with finance reports”
  3. AI + Vertical Industries — Education, healthcare, legal, e-commerce specific solutions
  4. Prompt Engineering Services — Short video scripts, poster design, resume optimization
  5. Digital Employee Services — Helping enterprises build AI-driven automation workflows

💡 Actionable Recommendations

Correct Startup Sequence

  1. Find the problem first — Start from “problems customers will pay to solve”
  2. Then build product — Services are the fastest cash flow and demand validator
  3. Run one small closed loop first — Includes acquisition, transaction, delivery, repurchase
  4. Then expand — Formalize company, build team after validation
