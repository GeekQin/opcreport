💡 Case Studies & Insights

Case 1: Li Jiehui’s Traditional Industry AI Transformation

Li Jiehui, an entrepreneur from a traditional industry, fully embraced AI last year. He uses AI for market research, code writing, design, copywriting, promotion, customer service, and data analysis. His entire business chain runs with one person plus AI. The key lesson: OPC’s core is not efficiency output, but capturing a demand that one person can satisfy—and getting paid for it.

Case 2: Liu Yefei’s 5 “AI Employees”

Liu Yefei, founder of Beijing Future Tidal Technology Co., spent 18 years in tech architecture and management at internet companies. Now he runs a company with 5 “AI employees” serving roles including:

  • Solution planning & decision support
  • Code writing
  • Code review
  • Cloud server automation deployment
  • Real-time information gathering & investment advice

🎯 Action Guide for Entrepreneurs

Three Core Elements of OPC Success

  1. Choose the Right Track: Content marketing, AI tool development, intelligent agent services, and creative industries are current hot directions
  2. Master AI Tools: Integrate AI deeply into product development, marketing, and customer service
  3. Focus on Demand: OPC’s core is “capturing a demand you can satisfy alone”—not pursuing comprehensive solutions

⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Many people make a fundamental mistake: assuming OPC requires mastering AI and controlling multiple AI Agents. This often leads to failure because they misunderstand OPC’s core—it should be demand-oriented, not technology-oriented.

🌐 Industry Trends & Outlook

OPC communities in 2026 are evolving from simple co-working spaces or startup communities into new organizational carriers connecting AI tools, individual entrepreneurs, industrial resources, and enterprise application scenarios. The value of OPC communities lies not only in “one person can start a business” but in “making individuals no longer start businesses in isolation.”

As WAIC 2026 approaches (July 17-20, Shanghai), the OPC track will undoubtedly gain新一轮 attention. The conference is expected to showcase more AI-empowered entrepreneurial innovation cases and provide more networking opportunities for one-person company entrepreneurs.
