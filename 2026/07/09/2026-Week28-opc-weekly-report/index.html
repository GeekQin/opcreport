Global OPC Co-Creation Festival Closes, Challenges Multiply: Solo Founders + AI Agents Are Rewriting the Rules of Startups

By Lao Qin Crypto — July 9, 2026, 20:04 (Asia/Shanghai) · Week 28

This week’s briefing is not about coin prices. It is about a new species quietly rewriting the meaning of “starting a company”: the One Person Company (OPC) — a single founder who, with a squad of AI agents, runs the entire business chain from product to market. The signal this week: OPC has moved from concept validation into ecosystem expansion, lifted simultaneously by policy, capital, and tooling.

1. Policy & Cities: OPC as a Hard Lever of “New Quality Productive Forces”

  • Hangzhou issued the Several Measures to Support OPC Innovation and Entrepreneurship for One-Person Companies (Hangzhou Market Regulation [2026] No. 43), giving OPC founders institutional backing on registration, premises, and tax services — one of the earliest government documents to explicitly endorse the one-person company model.
  • Qingdao launched the “Create in Qingdao” OPC Challenge (June 5) under the theme “Light Entrepreneurship Sets Sail,” open to founders under 45, targeting those planning to register a one-person entity in Qingdao or operating one less than a year old — no degree or industry restrictions.
  • Foshan (Greater Bay Area) offers a live case: Yaojing Culture founder Si Qingzheng uses a self-built AI platform to run the full pipeline of animated short dramas — script, image generation, and video output — solo.

2. Challenge Ecosystem: From Competitions to Certification

  • Global OPC Co-Creation Festival debuted at the 2026 Digital Economy Industry Expo in Beijing, closing July 4. As a signature板块 of the expo, it gathered one-person-company representatives across content marketing and music creation. China News Service noted that once technical barriers fall, one person can complete the full-chain work that once required a team.
  • WAIC × OpenCSG — OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge: billed as the world’s first top-tier incubation platform built for super-individuals and AI founders. The “Lingtuo LTOP” hackathon and South China semi-final roadshow was held in Shenzhen (Longgang) on May 30, positioning itself as a full ecosystem to “discover potential, link resources, and nurture future unicorns.”
  • SenseTime XiaoHuanXiong × Datawhale — China’s first OPC Capability Challenge: a RMB 550,000 prize pool, up to RMB 100,000 for the top individual, and the launch of the country’s first OPC capability certification system — turning “can you actually put AI into your workflow and ship real results” into a measurable standard.
  • “Huawei Cloud Cup” 2026 AI OPC Application Innovation Contest (Suzhou): launched April 23 in Suzhou Industrial Park, soliciting real-scenario AI applications across six industrial domains — next-gen IT, high-end equipment, biomedicine, nanomaterials, AI, and new energy.

3. Platforms & Communities: Infrastructure for the Solo Founder Is Taking Shape

  • OPC Community (opcchina.ai / xuanyuan): a one-stop “super-individual, AI-go” platform with 200+ ready-to-use skills, 30+ adapted models, a Skillhub marketplace, a city compute center (compute/training vouchers), and an entrepreneurship academy with certification.
  • OpenClaw OPC Solo-Company Evolution Bootcamp (Hangzhou): the first cohort, co-hosted by the AgentLai Group and OPC China, drew 160 entrepreneurs for a day-and-night intensive on agent-powered business.
  • BrainiacMinds (launched July 8): a Solo-CEO platform — “ONE PERSON. ENTIRE COMPANY.” — lets you train your first AI Employee in 15 seconds, with voice and avatar, handing reception and ops roles to digital staff.
  • Bika.ai (updated July 8): an “AI Organizer for One-Person Company” offering clone-and-install agentic app templates (AI Programmer, X/Twitter Manager, Lightweight CRM) so a solo founder can assemble a digital-employee matrix from templates.

4. Benchmark Cases: The “Revenue Proof” of Going Solo

  • Overseas benchmarks: Pieter Levels (Levelsio) runs 12 products solo at $300K+ monthly revenue; Marc Lou ships a new SaaS every two weeks with several at $10K+/mo; Danny Postma’s solo product tops $1M annual revenue.
  • Domestic samples: Tsinghua PhD Xu Zhanwei registered a one-person company in July 2025, joined Shenzhen Moliy Camp by October, and within half a year raised tens of millions for a smart wristband with two major orders. Nanjing University architecture graduate Shen Yinghao self-taught coding via AI and built the 3D game “Codename Xunshu” solo, carving a niche刚需 track with pure technical skill.

💡 Lao Qin’s take: The common thread is not “one person doing everything,” but one person designing task flows + AI agents + an external service network. For the first time, AI enlarges an individual’s manageable radius — you no longer have to hire before you can build.

5. Tech Foundation: The Agent Economy Is Here, and the Stack Is Mature

  • 2026 = the year of Agent action. The shift from “generative capability” to “agentic execution” is now structural. China’s enterprise-grade agent market is projected at RMB 44.9 billion, growing 107% YoY, with over 60% deployed on-premises. The AI law’s requirement for in-country private deployment of important data, plus privacy and latency needs, makes localization and customization刚需.
  • Reference “digital department” stack: product/UI via v0.dev, Lovable, bolt.new, heyboss, Replit (shippable landing pages in minutes); full-stack via Cursor (intent-driven development, non-coders can ship); domestic alternatives Coze.cn, Miaoda, Tongyi, DeepSeek-V3 (adapted, strong for code-gen); agent orchestration via Coze, Dify, OpenClaw, Bika.ai; efficiency via Raycast, Warp, Setapp, Recraft.

6. Cold Think: Boom, or the Biggest Trap?

⚠️ Reality check: OPC is sold as a freedom utopia, but practitioners reveal the brutal side — missing data sources, broken business loops, depleted cash flow. One analysis puts demo success at 95% but real production at just 50%. Stability, compliance, and cash flow are the real exams that decide whether an OPC survives year one.

  • Don’t bet on the “one person does it all” OPC; bet on “one person designs flows + AI agents + external networks.”
  • Beware the “easy money” narrative bubble manufactured by training institutes.
  • Legal, tax, and compliance risks are real — lean on the ecosystem (communities, incubators, compliance services), not lone heroics.

7. This Week’s Trend Conclusion

  1. Ecosystem-ization: OPC is evolving from lone wolf to a full loop of community + challenge + certification + compute; individuals no longer build alone.
  2. Platform-ization: dedicated solo-founder platforms (BrainiacMinds, Bika.ai, OPC Community) are emerging at once, lowering setup cost.
  3. Policy-ization: city-level challenges and dedicated measures keep escalating; OPC is written into multiple local “double innovation” strategies.
  4. Rational-ization: the hype of “anyone can do it” is giving way to “how do you survive” — the sector is entering its de-bubbling phase.
