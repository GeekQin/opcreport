1. OPC Standard Officially Released: Industry Definition Clarified

On July 9, 2026, China’s first “AI OPC Terminology” group standard was officially released, establishing an authoritative definition for the One-Person Company (OPC) sector:

Core Definition: An AI OPC refers to a company primarily engaged in AI technology research, development, application, or services, led and controlled by one core natural person, with typically no more than 10 employees. This core individual uses their personal brand and professional capabilities as core assets, leveraging intelligent agents to achieve independent operations and scalable delivery.

This standard marks the transition of OPC from concept to standardized development, providing clear criteria for entrepreneurs, investors, and policymakers.

7. Future Outlook

The OPC one-person company represents a new super-individual business model in the AI era. With the release of standards, major platform layouts, and the maturation of tool ecosystems, this entrepreneurship paradigm will enter a rapid development phase. However, entrepreneurs must also recognize:

  • Lower technology barriers ≠ lower success barriers
  • Personal brand and professional capabilities remain core competitiveness
  • AI is a lever, not a replacement
  • Sustainable operation requires systematic thinking and long-termism

Key Insight: The core of OPC lies not in “one person,” but in “how one person achieves scale with AI.” This requires entrepreneurs to possess business operation thinking, AI tool mastery capabilities, content IP building capabilities, and product service systematization capabilities.
