🧠 Domestic LLMs Rise: The Technical Foundation for OPCs

2026 has seen rapid development of domestic large language models (LLMs), bringing revolutionary changes to individual entrepreneurs. Alibaba Qwen, Xiaomi MiMo, Zhipu GLM-5V-Turbo and other Chinese models have been released consecutively, not only closing the gap with international top-tier models but also demonstrating unique advantages in localized application scenarios.

Three Major Improvements AI Brings to OPCs:

  1. Content Creation Efficiency Leap: From article writing to visual design, video editing, and voiceover production, AI tools are compressing multi-person collaborative workflows into a single individual’s fingertips. Marketing content that once took a week can now be drafted in a few hours.

  2. Customer Service Capacity at Scale: LLM-powered conversational systems enable one-person companies to achieve 24/7 customer response while maintaining high-quality service — traditionally only available to large enterprises.

  3. Data Analysis & Decision Support: The natural advantage of Chinese LLMs in localized understanding gives individual entrepreneurs access to professional business insights. AI is becoming the most reliable “strategist” for OPCs.
