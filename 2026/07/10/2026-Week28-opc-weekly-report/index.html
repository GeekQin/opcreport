📊 Key Findings

In 2026, AI tools have fully enabled the practice of One-Person Companies (OPC), making it a mature new entrepreneurship paradigm. Through the integrated use of AI super employees (AI Agents), automation toolchains, and cloud services, individual entrepreneurs can build and operate micro-enterprises covering the entire business chain with unprecedented efficiency. AI tools have transitioned from auxiliary roles to becoming programmable, collaborative, and capable of undertaking systematic tasks as core productivity units.

🔥 Hot Trends Overview

1️⃣ National Policy Dividend Matrix Accelerating

As of May 2026, China has formed an “One-Person Company (OPC)” policy dividend matrix led by Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen, with new first-tier cities like Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan, Xi’an, and Qingdao fully following suit:

Hangzhou, Zhejiang “Super Individual Capital” - Lowest startup threshold, supports “one address multiple registrations” and “desk registration”, zero registration cost

Chengdu, Sichuan Maximum subsidy up to 25 million CNY comprehensive subsidy

2️⃣ OPC Communities: From Physical Space to Digital Ecosystem

In 2026, OPC communities are evolving from simple office spaces or entrepreneurial groups into new organizational carriers connecting AI tools, individual entrepreneurs, industrial resources, and enterprise application scenarios. Using AI agents like OpenClaw and Miaoda, entrepreneurs are building vertical applications such as AI web dramas, enterprise intelligent sites, AI+HR, and AI content production through “single-person driven + AI collaboration”.

3️⃣ Digital Employee Era Officially Opens

2026 is called the “Digital Employee Year”. AI agents represented by “Lobster (OpenClaw)” have completely broken traditional code barriers. Technical “novices” no longer need to struggle learning programming languages - they can transform vague requirements into operable business applications through natural language dialogue.
