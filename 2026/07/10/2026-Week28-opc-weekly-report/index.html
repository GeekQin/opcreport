MIIT & 7 Ministries: Accelerating AI OPC Cultivation

On June 18, 2026, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, together with six other ministries including the Central Cyberspace Administration, National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Science and Technology, Ministry of Commerce, State Administration for Market Regulation, and National Data Administration, jointly issued the Action Plan for Promoting Platform Economy Large-Medium-Small Business Collaborative Development (2026–2028). The plan explicitly calls for “accelerating the cultivation of AI One-Person Companies (AI OPC)” and targets 100+ platform resource opening pilot projects by 2028. The plan also supports platforms in joint initiatives with SMEs focusing on Embodied Intelligence, Brain-Computer Interface, and other emerging industries.

Key focus areas include Agent development, AIGC product R&D, LLM fine-tuning, and industry AI applications, with a new credit product “OPC Loan” developed in partnership with financial institutions.
