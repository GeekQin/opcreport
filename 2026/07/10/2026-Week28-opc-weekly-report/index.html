🧠 Deep Dive: Why Did One-Person Companies Explode in 2026?

Technology Foundation: From “Support” to “Core”

2025’s collective upgrade in LLM capabilities enabled completely non-technical people to independently complete entire project development. AI is no longer just a “support tool” — it has become a programmable, collaborative, system-task-capable core productive unit. This is the fundamental reason for the OPC explosion in 2026.

Policy Dividend: From “Individual Exploration” to “National Strategy”

MIIT + seven ministries joint document, 20+ city-specific support policies, OPC incorporated into the “15th Five-Year Plan” — for the first time in history, one-person company entrepreneurship has been elevated to national strategic height. Batch incubation of “one-person companies” has moved from slogan to reality.

Business Logic: Return to the Essence of Business

Industry observer Frank provides two combat-proven recommendations:

  1. Build a personal traffic foundation
  2. Return to business essentials — anchor on real user needs

Technical barriers are no longer the core threshold. Super-individuals are not fighting alone — industry and community ecosystems bring peer networks, industry resources, and landing cooperation opportunities.

The Entrepreneurship Triangle: Skills + AI System + Personal IP

  • 角一：硬技能 (Hard Skills) — You must have a “scalpel” that solves specific problems
  • 角二：AI系统 (AI System) — Your “one-person army,” using AI to capture trends, build frameworks, generate materials
  • 角三：个人IP (Personal IP) — Your trust amplifier; content doesn’t need viral hits, just filters for like-minded people
