Trend Analysis

  • From “solo workshop” to “AI-native enterprise”: the consensus is shifting from “one person does all the work” to “one person designs task flows, AI runs them.”
  • Infrastructure year one: community (opc.community) + tools (Bika/BrainiacMinds) + orchestration (Claude Code OPC Skill) + competition (WAIC Challenge) are all in place — OPC enters a replicable stage.
  • Policy-arbitrage window: Shenzhen/Beijing near-zero-cost launch incentives remain the most certain “low-cost validation” opportunity for H2 2026.
  • Reflexive talent growth: AI Agent roles pay ~50% more, have ~30% more openings and ~70% less competition — OPC both creates and absorbs jobs.

⚠️ Risk Reminders

  • OPC is not risk-free entrepreneurship. Revenue cases follow a long-tail distribution; media amplifies the top samples, so ordinary individuals must prepare cash flow and compliance.
  • AI tools depend on third-party APIs, exposing you to cost volatility, policy and shutdown risk; be wary of “automatic passive income” pitches.
  • A one-person company still carries legal-person liability and tax compliance — confirm address, supervisor and capital-injection requirements before registering.
