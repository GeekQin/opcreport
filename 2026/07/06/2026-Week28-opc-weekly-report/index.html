1. Policy Tailwinds: From Local Pilot to National Instrument

OPC’s policy dividends are upgrading from isolated city experiments to a national-level industrial instrument. The revised Company Law has lifted the restriction that “one natural person may only own one one-person company,” providing the registration bedrock for AI-driven micro-ventures. The 2026 Government Work Report explicitly calls for “deepening and expanding ‘AI+’.”

  • Guangdong — Provincial OPC action plan (2026–2030) supports AI and future-industry OPCs; an earlier (2026–2028) AI-OPC plan encourages banks to launch OPC-specific financial products such as “compute loans” and “talent loans.” (Source: Sina Finance / Guangdong Gov, 2026-05-15)
  • Shanghai (Pudong) — Newly registered AI OPCs get up to ¥300K free compute plus a “Ten-Ones” dream-start package (free desks, talent apartments, funding, compute vouchers, scenario matchmaking), targeting 1,000+ new firms and ¥250B AI output within three years. (Source: Penguin, 2025-12-13)
  • Suzhou / Shenzhen / Hangzhou — Suzhou offers up to ¥2M/year compute subsidy; Shenzhen up to ¥10M equity coinvestment and an “AI Pioneer City” push; Hangzhou invests ¥100M/year in special funds; Chengdu tops out at ¥25M. 20+ cities now run dedicated OPC policies. (Source: OSCHINA / CSDN, 2026-05)

3. Tooling: The “AI Super-Employee” Stack for One Person

The core enabler of OPC is a mature tool chain. The full loop from “write code” to “get paid” can now be assembled by one person using cloud services:

  • The 5-piece standard stack for a one-person AI product — Cursor/Lovable (build), Supabase (data + auth), Lemon Squeezy (payments + tax remittance), Plausible (analytics), Resend (email). Common trait: free tiers are enough before you earn. (Source: Tencent Cloud Dev, 2026-07-06)
  • One person managing 47 AI Agents: AgentHub’s “Harness Engineering” — An open-source Electron app that orchestrates 47 agents (dev, backend, design) with hooks enforcing quality and skills accumulating experience, turning “managing a virtual team” into an operable engineering system. (Source: GitHub / Stanshy, 2026-06-17)
  • Tool lists exploding: awesome-solopreneur-ai lists 367+ tools — Across 19 categories, from AI writing and design to video, customer service, bookkeeping, and SEO. Bika.ai positions itself directly as the “AI Organizer for One-Person Company,” offering multi-agent collaboration and workflow templates. (Source: GitHub, 2026-04-15)

💡 Editor’s note: What used to take 3–5 people now runs with 1 person + models + automation. The real dividing line is not “who knows AI,” but “who can wire AI into the business flow and turn it into deliverable results.” That is the OPC moat.

4. Three Core Trends: Globalization · OPC-ization · Agent-ization

  • Globalization (going global) — Overseas users pay better and competition is thinner. Multiple Chinese-built AI writing, image, and video tools report stable monthly revenue on App Store / Stripe; localization tweaks can lift conversion 3x+. Playbook: Chinese MVP → translate + localize → launch overseas, focusing on Shopify plugins, TikTok content, and indie-site SEO. (Source: SegmentFault / CSDN, 2026-02~05)
  • Agent-ization: from “tool” to “partner” — Agents shift from passive response to active execution — understanding goals, decomposing tasks, calling tools, iterating continuously. Vertical-scenario “ready-to-use” lightweight SaaS sold by monthly subscription is the steadiest monetization path. (Source: SegmentFault, 2026-02-27)
  • OPC-ization: single-founder share up 50%+ in six years — Carta data shows single-founder new companies rose from 23.7% (2019) to 36.3% (H1 2025). Sam Altman has a betting pool on “the year the first one-person billion-dollar company appears” — no longer unthinkable. (Source: Carta 2025 / OpenAI)

⚠️ AI scams surged +1,210% YoY; Q1 2026 saw 78,000 tech jobs cut. The EU AI Act takes effect August 2026 — any AI product serving EU users must pre-assess compliance. OPCs are “small and agile” but have “no compliance team to fall back on.” Design data privacy, content safety, and tax remittance (e.g., Lemon Squeezy’s sales-tax collection) in from day one.
