💡 What is OPC (One-Person Company)?

Core Formula

OPC (One Person Company) represents a major transformation in business organization. With AI technology empowerment, individual entrepreneurs can independently operate a complete business loop—from product development and marketing to customer service, achieving full-chain intelligent management.

OPC Core Characteristics

Feature Description Counter-example
👤 Solo Operation Built & maintained by 1 person (max 5 team) Large-scale team projects
💵 Sustainable Revenue Stable cash flow (MRR/ARR) Pure open-source/hobby projects
🤖 Leverage Effect Relies on software/content/automation Consulting/labor-intensive services
🎨 Autonomous Control Pursues freedom & autonomy VC-driven high-growth companies

Core Philosophy: Build a life, not just a company.

🌟 Super Individual Success Stories

Case 1: AI Origin Community — Lingzhou Technology

In November last year, Feng Huanyuan decided to stop being an entrepreneurship “mentor” and joined the AI Origin Community to start Lingzhou Technology, focused on AI-native hardware development. Their first product is an “AI Sleep Cycle Assistant” that uses sensors to detect mood changes and generate real-time sleep music and natural sounds with lighting effects for immersive sleep experiences. Lingzhou Technology currently has only 4 core team members but produces work equivalent to a 20-person team.

Case 2: Tsinghua Kelly — No-Code Product in 1 Week

Kelly, a product manager focused on user growth at Tsinghua University, with zero coding background, independently developed a simplified Bing Search product using ChatGPT and Copilot in just 1 week for about $5. “After human-AI interaction, you have the capabilities of a front-end engineer, middle-tier developer, back-end engineer, and an entire team.”

Case 3: AI Music One-Person Company

A female entrepreneur in the AI music field has no permanent employees but efficiently integrates the entire chain of creation, production, and delivery through AI tools and global collaboration networks. Her response speed far exceeds traditional music companies when urgent projects need quick turnaround.

Case 4: Traditional Industry Transformation — AI Legal SaaS

An entrepreneur who embraced AI last year built an intelligent contract review system for a law firm and a comprehensive electronic task management system for a business consulting company. This individual + AI model breaks traditional barriers of team, capital, and venue requirements.

📈 Three Major OPC Trends in 2026

Trend 1: Extreme Vertical Specialization

Super individuals no longer pursue broad markets but lock onto specific niche markets, improving product and service quality through AI. Examples include elderly health management, intelligent contract review, vertical AI applications. This focus increases success rates and professionalizes the market.

Trend 2: Strong Collaboration

Different from traditional solo entrepreneurship, modern “super individuals” emphasize collaboration. Building ecosystems together creates resource sharing. AI tech companies partner with traditional industry entrepreneurs to empower digital transformation.

Trend 3: Zero-Cost Entrepreneurship Environment

Using open-source tools, cloud platforms, and social media promotion, they achieve transformation from nothing. DeepSeek and other open-source models have lowered AI costs significantly. Zero-cost entrepreneurship enables more people to participate in the entrepreneurial wave.
