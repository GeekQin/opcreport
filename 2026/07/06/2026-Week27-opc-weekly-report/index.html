In 2026, AI Agents will enter the scale deployment phase. Five core trends include multi-agent collaboration, enterprise-scale deployment, vertical industry specialization, improved trustworthiness and transparency, and human-machine collaboration restructuring.

📝 OPC Entrepreneurship: The Hardest Part Isn’t Technology

What’s fresh isn’t the technology itself, but the production logic behind it—one person plus one set of AI tool chain can independently complete the full-chain business loop from creative R&D to market operations and customer service, achieving true single-person army super individual.

⚠️ Stay Cool About “Get Rich Quick” Narratives

Stories of one-person companies earning millions annually circulate in OPC communities. However, as the OPC model evolves, “one-person” doesn’t mean literally one person—it refers to super individuals or super teams empowered by AI. Most entrepreneurs still struggle with business challenges. The real出路 (way out) is shifting from human-driven traffic buying to agent-driven autonomous customer acquisition.
