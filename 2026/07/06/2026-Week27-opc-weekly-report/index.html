🛠️ Tools & Practical Methodology

OPC Getting Started Guide: How AI Tools Enable “One Person Does the Work of Many”

OPC (One Person Company) core proposition is “one person runs a small but beautiful individual business.” AI tools have made a significant leap from “AI helps you write a paragraph” to “AI helps you complete an entire task”:

Task Traditional AI-Powered
Coding Code completion AI completes entire feature development (Claude Code, Cursor)
Marketing Generate marketing copy One-click produces complete publishing materials
Research Search result lists AI directly provides research reports with citations
Finance Manual bookkeeping AI auto-classification + report generation + tax advice

What previously required “Marketing Department + Tech Department + Design Department + Finance Department” can now run with one person + an AI tool stack.

OPC AI Marketing Agent Practice: Reconstructing Autonomous Customer Acquisition

In 2026, OPC has become a new commercial species of “single person + AI agent clusters.” For resource-constrained OPCs, the real path is shifting from human-driven traffic purchasing to agent-driven autonomous customer acquisition—building a dynamic growth engine that senses signals, validates needs, and accumulates assets.

📅 June 30, 2026 | Source: CSDN
