🔥 Hot Events This Week

🏛️ Beijing Haidian Showcases 30+ OPC Enterprises at 2026 Digital Economy Expo

Haidian District of Beijing showcased approximately 30+ OPC (One Person Company) entrepreneurial representatives at this year’s expo, presenting an innovation ecosystem for “super individuals” in the AI era. Among the featured projects, “Dragon Bone Animation” platform developed solely by Su Kui, founder of Nezha Entertainment, can generate 2D skeletal animations with a single sentence prompt and recently won the first prize at the “Entrepreneurship Beijing” OPC Special Competition.

Tags: #Beijing #PolicySupport #Expo

🌴 Hainan Free Trade Port Launches AI OPC Special Support Policy

Four Hainan provincial departments jointly issued an AI OPC one-person company special support policy, officially implemented from June 20, 2026, valid until end of 2028. Core policy: Select 1-3 high-quality OPC ecosystem communities annually province-wide, offering a maximum ¥8 million one-time financial reward; plus FTZ tax, cross-border, and venue benefits.

Tags: #Hainan #Policy #HighRewards

🏆 WAIC and OpenCSG Launch OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge

The World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC), jointly with OpenCSG, launched the OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge, becoming the world’s first top-tier incubation platform specifically designed for super individuals and AI entrepreneurs. The competition aims to discover and nurture new entrepreneurial forces in the AI era.

Tags: #WAIC #StartupCompetition #Incubation

🌆 Shenzhen Releases OPC Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Action Plan

Shenzhen issued the “Shenzhen Action Plan for Building an AI OPC Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Leadership Hub (2026-2027)” in 2026, proposing to build over 10 OPC communities by end of 2027, cultivate over 1,000 high-growth AI startups, and gather over 10,000 AI innovation and entrepreneurship talents.

Type 2: Vertical Niche - Deep Dive into Segments

Fudan graduate, resigned from state-owned enterprise, registered one-person company. Used AI to compress article writing time from 2-3 hours to under 1 hour. Income surpassed original salary within two months.

📋 OPC Entrepreneurship Practical Guide

▎ Run Your First Cash Flow in 90 Days

Core Principle: Find problems first, then build products; sell services first, then create standardized products

Step 1 (Days 1-30): Validatable Billable Problem

  • Start from “problems customers will pay to solve” not “what I want to build”
  • Use AI for industry research and competitor analysis
  • Find 1 essential pain point in a niche sector

Step 2 (Days 31-60): Service Model Validation

  • Sell services first for fastest cash flow and demand validation
  • Use AI to mass-produce multi-platform content drafts
  • Build FAQ knowledge base + automated customer service

Step 3 (Days 61-90): Productization & Expansion

  • Run through 1 complete loop first, then expand
  • One loop contains: Acquisition → Transaction → Delivery → Repurchase
  • Standardize successful experience into digital products

▎ Common OPC Revenue Models

🎯 Industry Expert Insights

“OPC is not solo fighting, but one person with CEO mindset, integrating AI tools, outsourced collaboration, and platform resources, independently completing the full commercial loop from ‘demand insight → product delivery → revenue realization’.”

Different from freelancing: Freelancers ‘sell time for compensation’, getting paid once per task; OPC ‘sells systems for compound interest’, with capability models, customer networks, and delivery processes accumulating scale effects over time.

“You’re not doing all the work alone, but ‘one person coordinating multiple digital employees’.”

Key transformation: From “what I do” to “what I let AI do”. Assign research, content, sales, customer service, and operations roles to AI, while you focus on decision-making and optimization.
