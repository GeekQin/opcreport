Executive Summary

The one-person company (OPC) model has evolved from a theoretical concept to a validated business paradigm in 2026. The critical insight: OPC success isn’t about one person doing everything—it’s about one person designing task flows and orchestrating AI Agents, automation tools, and external service networks to execute those tasks. AI has fundamentally expanded the “manageable scope” for individuals, transforming what previously required team building into solopreneur-powered micro-enterprises.

Key Statistics

  • 16 million one-person limited liability companies in China as of June 2025 (27.4% of all enterprises)
  • 47% YoY growth in new OPC registrations in H1 2025
  • 90% of indie developers start with less than $500 in initial capital
  • Development efficiency formula: P_efficiency = (I × A^n) / C where A = AI leverage ratio, n = synergy coefficient

In 2026, OPC communities are evolving from simple co-working spaces or entrepreneur groups into new organizational carriers connecting AI tools, individual entrepreneurs, industrial resources, and enterprise application scenarios. The value lies not just in “one person can start a business,” but in ensuring individuals no longer start businesses in isolation.

Community Functions

  • AI tool integration and training
  • Industrial resource matching
  • Enterprise application scenario incubation

  • AI Training: Monthly revenue exceeding 1 million RMB

  • Vertical Industry AI Solutions: Targeting education, healthcare, legal, e-commerce scenarios
