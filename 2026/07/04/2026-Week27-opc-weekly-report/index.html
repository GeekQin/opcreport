⚠️ VI. Three Traps for Small-Team AI Agent Startups

Recent deep analysis identifies three major engineering pitfalls:

  1. Architecture Trap: Mistaking “automation workflows” for “intelligent agents” — Agents without RAG knowledge bases and Tool Use capabilities are just scripts

  3. Cost Trap: The ongoing cost of LLM API calls is severely underestimated. Solo developers need solid cost planning.
