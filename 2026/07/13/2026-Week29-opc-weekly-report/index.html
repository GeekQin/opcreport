2. Policy Radar

In 2026, OPC support moved from “paper slogans” to “real cash,” forming three policy hubs plus several specialty nodes:

  • Shenzhen — Action Plan to Build an AI OPC Startup Ecosystem Leader (2026–2027): city-level vouchers up to ¥10M “training-compute voucher,” ¥2M “model voucher,” ¥2M “corpus voucher”; builds an “upstairs-is-upstream” industrial chain community.
  • Hainan — first provincial OPC policy (effective 6/20): dual 15% tax (15% corporate income tax, 15% capped personal income tax for high-end talent), “smart consumption vouchers” subsidizing compute/models, up to ¥10M unsecured credit loan + 1% interest discount, ≥200 rent-free workstations.
  • Suzhou / Wujiang — dedicated “Lobster Service Zone” deploys OpenClaw-class agents for free; ¥2B AI direct-investment fund, ¥10B dedicated credit, up to ¥1,500/month rent subsidy for 3 years; goal of 30+ OPC communities by 2028.
  • Changzhou / Wuxi / Shanghai — Changzhou opened 1,000 free cloud seats + ¥100M fund; Wuxi Hi-Tech Zone offers up to 3 years “zero rent”; Shanghai Xuhui offers up to ¥100K start funding + ¥1M compute/model voucher + free workspace & accounting.
  • Institutional base — from 2026-01-01, the revised Company Law removes the “one natural person = one OPC” limit; registered capital paid within 5 years; still limited liability; small-scale VAT taxpayers exempt below ¥100K monthly sales.

⚠️ Watch-out: Many “up to ¥8M rewards” are NOT paid directly to founders — they go to qualifying OPC communities (Hainan picks only 1–3 per year) and flow back via smart vouchers / rent-free seats. Read the fine print on who gets it and how.

7. Cold Truth & Risks

⚠️ Not every OPC makes money. Research shows profitable OPCs cluster in three types: ① executors who wire AI into real business flows; ② “expert” individuals in extremely vertical niches; ③ people who use AI to amplify their ability, not chase a fantasy “money printer.” AI is a lever, not a printer — it amplifies your strength and your blind spots.

  • Compute/Token is a hidden cost — running 17 agents racks up a real token bill; unit economics must be calculated.
  • Platform-dependency risk — multi-platform side hustles are directly exposed to platform audits, throttling, and rule changes.
  • “No-human company” compliance gap — purely agent-autonomous content/transactions remain a gray zone on liability, tax, and consumer rights.
