💡 2026 One-Person Company 6 Major Tracks Analysis

Track 1: Digital Content & Creative Services

AI tools have completely revolutionized traditional production processes. Script writing, character design, storyboard generation and other stages have achieved automation. An independent creator spent three months completing an 88-minute AI short drama, operated by a single person, with costs controlled at the tens of thousands of yuan level.

Track 2: AI Application/Tool Development

When AI reduces coding barriers to zero, even those who don’t understand code can independently complete entire project development. Among the tracks most suitable for one-person companies in 2026, AI tool development has medium difficulty but extremely high ceiling. Companies like Lovable achieving $100M ARR in 8 months is the best example.

Track 3: Vertical Domain Consulting/Services

After AI appears, production costs for content, code, and design approach zero. The core logic for track selection is: use AI to amplify your original advantages. Relying solely on “using AI to produce content” is no longer enough — you need industry experience, human resources, and deep cognition as your moat.

Track 4: Cross-border E-commerce/Overseas Services

From Beijing to Zhejiang, from Jiangsu to Shandong, regions across the country are extending olive branches to global AI geeks, digital nomads, and hardcore entrepreneurs. This new personal + AI entrepreneurship model breaks the traditional barriers of team, capital, and space requirements.

Track 5: AI Hardware & Vertical Applications

At CES 2026, Chinese companies became the focus, opening the “Physical AI” era. The combination of AI and hardware has become a new trend, with individual entrepreneurs able to enter specific vertical domains.

Track 6: Education/Knowledge Monetization

The core principle is: build once, sell many times. When products can be automatically delivered, automatically renewed, and automatically supported, founders transition from “trading time for money” to “trading systems for money.”
