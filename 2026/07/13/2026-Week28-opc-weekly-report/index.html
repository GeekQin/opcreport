1. Major Event: First WorkBuddy OPC Community Launches in Chengdu

On July 10, 2026, China’s first Tencent WorkBuddy OPC cooperative community was officially established at the Tianfu Changdao Digital Cultural Innovation Park in Chengdu High-tech Zone. This community, jointly initiated by Tencent Cloud and Chengdu High-tech Cultural and Creative Media Co., aims to explore a tripartite collaborative OPC growth path featuring “government support, platform support, and community co-creation.”

Five Support Dimensions

The community provides comprehensive support through five key dimensions:

  • Productivity Foundation: Exclusive accounts and computing power credits for resident OPCs to reduce AI usage costs
  • Tool Empowerment: Full suite of AI toolchains via the WorkBuddy platform
  • Ecosystem Connection: Access to industry resources and partner networks
  • Long-term Support: Expert guidance and practical training camps
  • Value Amplification: Brand exposure and business opportunity matching

WorkBuddy Platform Positioning

WorkBuddy is Tencent’s self-developed AI intelligent workbench that allows creators to connect the entire workflow on a single platform—from idea organization, material collection, content generation, visual design, and project management to promotional materials and business plans—breaking down the silos between different positions in traditional creation.

4. Business Model: Evolution from Super Individual to OPC

Core OPC Definition

The standard architecture of OPC (One Person Company) is “1 core decision-maker + N AI digital employees + ∞ global collaboration network.” This is not a traditional “freelancer upgrade” but the smallest practice unit of an AI-native enterprise.

Key Differences

  • Freelancer: “Solo combat,” relying on selling personal time and skills for monetization; income stops when work stops
  • OPC: Possesses independent legal entity status, with accumulable business assets and credit systems

Typical Industry Distribution

  • AI tool development and SaaS subscription services
  • AI content generation and knowledge monetization
  • Cross-border e-commerce and digital product sales
  • Software services and API call charges

5. Innovation Practice: Hive Organization Model Exploration

The latest “Hive Organization Model” is exploring how to build a symbiotic business ecosystem from 100 “one-person companies.” This model achieves scaled collaboration through OPC autonomy, breaking the stereotype that one-person companies must fight alone.

Core Features

  • Taking super individuals or IPs as trust entry points
  • Connecting resources and undertaking business
  • Completing high-quality delivery with small, lean collaborative teams
  • Sharing infrastructure and business opportunities
