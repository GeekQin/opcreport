Industry Insight

The real difficulty in 2026 OPC entrepreneurship is not technology implementation, but building a commercial closed loop. Profitable OPCs mainly concentrate in three categories:

  1. Tech experts - crushing competition with superior skills
  2. Niche verticals - deep cultivation of specific sectors
  3. Service-type OPC - AI-powered efficiency enhancement
