💡 What is OPC, Really?

The Core Definition

OPC (One-Person Company) is a new entrepreneurial form where a single individual, powered by AI technology, independently completes the full business loop — product design, development, operations, customer service — all without a traditional team.

The formula: Carbon-based intelligence (human) + Silicon-based execution (AI) = A complete company.

The Five Defining Characteristics

Characteristic What It Means
Digital Native Built on digital infrastructure and AI tools from day one — no physical office needed
Multi-Role Capability AI tool chain enables one person to function as PM, developer, operator, marketer, and support
10x Efficiency AI handles repetitive/standardized work; founder focuses on strategy and creative decisions
Ecosystem-Dependent Deep reliance on AI tools, cloud platforms, API economy, and external infrastructure
Scenario-Driven Products built around specific niches, with rapid iteration and precision positioning

The Three OPC Models

  1. Innovation Model — Disrupt traditional workflows with AI. Example: AI-driven personalized education, smart customer service outsourcing
  2. Scenario Application Model — Build targeted AI solutions for vertical pain points. Example: AI brand visibility detection
  3. Ecosystem Service Model — Provide tools, computing, or training to other OPCs. Example: AI agent deployment services

The Monetization Escalator

Stage 1 — Project Services: Use AI tools to take on outsourcing projects, validate demand, build experience
Stage 2 — Productized Services: Package proven approaches into standardized products (SaaS tools, courses, templates)
Stage 3 — Platform Play: Build a two-sided marketplace matching supply and demand — passive income, true scale
