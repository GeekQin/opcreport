1. China’s First OPC Terminology Standard Officially Published

Date: July 9, 2026 | Source: Zhejiang Digital Economy Development Center

The country’s first-ever terminology standard for AI OPC (One-Person Company) was officially published, drafted jointly by 14 organizations and led by the Zhejiang Digital Economy Development Center. It will take effect on August 1, 2026.

Key definitions from the standard:

  • AI OPC: A company controlled by 1 core natural person with ≤10 employees, focused on AI R&D, applications, or services
  • Super Individual (超级个体): A core natural person who leverages AI to exceed natural capacity limits and delivers business value that normally requires a team
  • Core characteristics: Highly specialized domain knowledge · Ability to orchestrate multiple AI agents · Consistent high-value output capability

Most significant change: The definition threshold shifted from “≤3 shareholders, ≤20 employees” to “1 dominant person, ≤10 employees”.
