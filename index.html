1. From “Human + Agent” to “Human + Autonomous Company”: The Three-Stage Agent Leap

The industry broadly accepts a clear evolution path: 2025 was “human + intelligent agents” (single-task automation); 2026 enters “human + agent swarms” (multi-workflow collaboration); looking toward 2027, it is “human + autonomous company” — a corporate entity that makes its own strategic decisions, optimizes operations, and responds to the market. The 2026 “OPC AI stack” has become relatively standardized:

  • Chief Architect: Cursor (Agent Mode) / Devin — full-stack code generation and refactoring
